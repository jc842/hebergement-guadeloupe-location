# Eco-Lodges & Rainforest Bungalows in Basse-Terre: Sustainable Island Stays

For travelers seeking quiet tranquility far removed from bustling beach resorts, Basse-Terre's rainforest eco-lodges provide an unforgettable immersion into tropical nature.

---

## 1. Architecture Harmonized with Nature
Guadeloupe's premier eco-lodges prioritize traditional Antillean timber craftsmanship:
- Open-air wooden verandas oriented to catch natural mountain trade winds.
- Rainwater harvesting systems and solar water heating.
- Natural wood materials (teak, red cedar, courbaril) seamlessly blending into the lush forest canopy.

## 2. Top Green Retreat Locations
- **Pointe-Noire & Bouillante**: Nestled between tropical rivers and the Cousteau Marine Reserve, surrounded by bamboo groves and vanilla orchids.
- **Saint-Claude & Matouba**: High up the slopes of La Soufrière, where cool nights and morning mist offer fresh respite.
- **Vieux-Habitants**: Historic coffee-growing valleys offering stays on active agro-ecological estates.

## 3. Activities Direct from Your Doorstep
Wake up to the melodic song of tree frogs (*hylodes*) and hummingbirds, step onto private rainforest trails leading to hidden river pools, and harvest fresh tropical fruits on-site.
