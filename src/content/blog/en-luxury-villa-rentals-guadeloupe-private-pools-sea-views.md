# Luxury Villa Rentals in Guadeloupe: Private Infinity Pools, Sea Views & Concierge

From contemporary minimalist oceanfront estates in Saint-François to secluded tropical timber hideaways in Deshaies, Guadeloupe offers an elite portfolio of private vacation properties.

---

## 1. Prime Locations for Luxury Villas
- **Saint-François**: Known for waterfront villas with private docks, lagoon views, and walking distance to the international 18-hole Robert Trent Jones golf course.
- **Sainte-Anne**: High-end gated residential domains overlooking turquoise lagoons, featuring lush bougainvillea gardens and expansive shaded verandas.
- **Deshaies**: Clifftop timber villas boasting panoramic Caribbean Sea views, breathtaking sunsets, and private infinity plunge pools nestled in primary greenery.

## 2. Essential Luxury Amenities
- **Architectural Design**: Seamless indoor-outdoor living (*varangues*), ventilated cathedral ceilings, and native teak woodwork.
- **Private Pools & Spa Facilities**: Infinity pools (*piscine à débordement*), heated outdoor Jacuzzis, and sun decks with lounge cabanas.
- **Bespoke Services**: Dedicated property concierges offering private Caribbean chef dining, daily housekeeping, boat charters, and in-villa massage therapy.

## 3. Direct Booking Advice
Booking directly with local villa managers avoids heavy OTA service fees (often saving 15% to 20%) while ensuring personalized itinerary support upon arrival.
