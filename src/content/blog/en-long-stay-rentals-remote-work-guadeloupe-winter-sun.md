# Long-Stay Rentals & Remote Work in Guadeloupe: Winter Sun Living Guide

As remote working gains widespread adoption, Guadeloupe has emerged as an attractive winter destination for European professionals and retirees.

---

## 1. The French Caribbean Advantage
- **European Infrastructure**: As a French Overseas Department (DOM), Guadeloupe operates under European Union legal frameworks, consumer protections, and top-tier healthcare facilities (CHU de Pointe-à-Pitre).
- **High-Speed Fiber Internet**: Fiber-optic broadband (*la fibre*) is widely deployed across coastal towns and residential rental villas.
- **Convenient Time Zone**: Operating at UTC-4 (only 5 or 6 hours behind Paris/Western Europe), allowing easy coordination with European business hours in the morning.

## 2. Negotiating Monthly Rental Rates
- **Off-Peak & Shoulder Seasons**: Substantial discounts (30% to 50% off weekly rates) are readily available from May to November.
- **Utility Inclusions**: Ensure your rental agreement clearly specifies electricity (air conditioning usage) and water allowances.

## 3. Best Long-Stay Hubs
Choose **Le Gosier** or **Baie-Mahault** for central access to coworking facilities and shopping, or **Saint-François** for after-work ocean swims and marina dining.
